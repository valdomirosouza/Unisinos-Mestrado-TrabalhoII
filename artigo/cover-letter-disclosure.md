**Originality and exclusive submission.** This manuscript is original,
has not been published, and is not under consideration by any other
journal or conference. No part of it has been posted as a preprint.
`[CONFIRMAR: nenhum preprint foi publicado]`

**Prior partial disclosure.** In line with the disclosure duties of
CNPq Ordinance No. 2,664/2026 (Arts. 9-II-e and 9-II-u), we declare the
following prior outputs related to this work:

1. This review updates a foundational systematic review (19 included
   studies) produced by the first author as graduate coursework in the
   Applied Computing Graduate Program (PPGCA) at Unisinos ("Trabalho
   I"). That work was submitted for internal academic evaluation only
   and was not formally published. The present manuscript is a
   substantially new study --- three registered update cycles, 126
   candidate studies, 65 included --- and discloses the inherited
   foundational corpus explicitly (Section II-C and the PRISMA flow).
   `[CONFIRMAR: o Trabalho I não foi apresentado ou depositado publicamente]`
2. The review's audit trail was maintained in a private Git repository
   during execution and is being made public as a replication package
   deposited at Zenodo (DOI: `[PREENCHER]`), referenced in the
   manuscript's Data Availability section.
3. No other public presentation or electronic dissemination of this
   content has occurred. `[CONFIRMAR]`

**No duplicate or fragmented publication.** The results of this review
are reported in full in this single manuscript; no part of them has
been or will be submitted separately for publication (CNPq Ordinance
No. 2,664/2026, Art. 9-II-f).

**Generative AI disclosure.** In compliance with CNPq Ordinance
No. 3,004/2026 (Art. 4) and the journal's AI policy, every use of
generative AI is declared in the manuscript's _Declaration of
Generative AI Use_ section: three supervised LLM assistants (Claude,
ChatGPT, Gemini) drafted full-text assessments under an LLM-assisted,
human-decided protocol, and Claude assisted manuscript preparation; all
screening decisions and final verification were performed by the
authors, who take full responsibility for the content.

**Authorship, competing interests, and funding.** Both authors meet the
authorship criteria, approved this version, and agree to its
submission; individual contributions are stated in the manuscript
(Author Contributions, CRediT). Competing interests are declared in the
manuscript (Conflict of Interest); the work received no specific grant.
