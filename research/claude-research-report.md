## Key Findings
- The richest net-new vein is **LLM/agentic root-cause analysis and remediation for cloud-native systems**. Much of this work lives in conference proceedings rather than journals, so honest flagging — not exclusion — is the right call given your user-confirmed defaults.
- Only a small set is simultaneously (a) journal-published, (b) SJR Q1/Q2, and (c) clearly on-topic: TAMO, ARM, the ESWA AgentAI survey, the IJIS incident-management study, the Wiley IJNM survey, the Scientific Reports cyber-range MAS, and the IEEE *Computer* "Cyber Arms Race" column.
- **Quantitative copilot-efficacy evidence (T8/RQ3)** in peer-reviewed sources is genuinely thin. ARM (closed-loop RCA + remediation through an MCP server) and a low-tier IaC self-healing paper (reported mean time to remediation of 6.9 minutes) are the most concrete; the latter's venue quality is weak.
- A real dedup hazard: **Kshetri, "Transforming cybersecurity with agentic AI…"** exists both as the SSRN item already in your corpus (P14) and as a *Telecommunications Policy* article (DOI 10.1016/j.telpol…, ScienceDirect S0308596125000734). I treat the journal version as the **same study as P14** and exclude it to honour the 0-duplicate requirement.
- Several widely-cited survey hits were screened out as P-corpus overlaps: "A Survey of AIOps in the Era of Large Language Models" (= P5), and Acharya et al. / Sapkota et al. agentic-AI surveys (= P10 / P9).
